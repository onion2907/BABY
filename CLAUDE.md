# How to communicate with the owner of this repo

They are not a technical person.

At the end of every piece of work, finish with a short section headed
**What you need to do next**, written in plain English:

- Numbered steps, in the order they should be done.
- Say what to click, type, or open — not how it works internally.
- No jargon. If a technical word is unavoidable, say in half a sentence
  what it means.
- Say plainly if there is nothing for them to do.
- Keep technical detail out of this section entirely. If it is worth
  saying at all, put it above under its own heading, so it can be skipped.
